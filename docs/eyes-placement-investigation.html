04 / Geometry contradicts the caption

An ascending step does not
preserve the same corner contact.

R1 formula versus R1 illustration

The R1 caption says a returning course can rise while preserving identical exit-to-entry corner contact. Its own equations disagree:

previous.end.y = previous.start.y + D
next.start.y   = previous.start.y − D

next.start.y − previous.end.y = −2D

The corners are separated by 2D, not coincident. At the defaults, the boundary edges overlap by that amount.

Proposed correction. Describe R1 as a provisional descending scaffold whose contacts may change during alignment and packing. To make ascending actions preserve the same physical corner connection, the cell or crossing orientation must also change.

The invariant’s exception should include R3 and R4: packing and role-change anchors can move cells belonging to different ribbon runs.

Settle the rules,
then update the implementation.

  1. Retain the clarified R3 scope.
    Exact contact applies only to consecutive same-lean column events. The original point 1 is withdrawn; no R3 relaxation is needed.
  2. Make R4’s selection policy explicit.
    Preserve R2 and the selected hard R3 conditions. Admit midpoint requests only when compatible with all accepted constraints.

    Use the current boundary geometry to derive the proposed shift. Measure final midpoint placement separately from admission status.
  4. Correct R1 and refresh the evidence.
    Update the ascending-course claim, precedence prose, sample coverage, and event-based regression fixtures.
